03 — How a project runs

No surprises.

Fixed price when the scope is clear. A short paid discovery when it isn't.

  1. 1

    Discover

    A call, then a one-page brief with a price and a timeline.

  2. 2

    Design

    A mock-up before any code. You react to pictures, not promises.

  3. 3

    Build

    Staging first. Tests on anything that touches money or data.

  4. 4

    Keep it alive

    Deploys, monitoring, backups, docs. A retainer, or a clean hand-off.
